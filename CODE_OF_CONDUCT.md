# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to maintaining a welcoming, respectful, and constructive environment for every contributor—pastors, researchers, technologists, students, and community members—regardless of background, experience level, or geography.

## Expected Behavior

- **Grace & Truth:** Communicate with courtesy, humility, and charitable intent in issues and pull requests.
- **Accuracy First:** Verify URLs, institutional names, and doctrinal citations before opening a PR.
- **Constructive Collaboration:** Focus feedback on factual accuracy, link health, and clarity of organization.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, personal attacks, trolling, or derogatory comments.
- Spam, unrelated commercial self-promotion, or malicious/broken links.
- Publishing private contact details of church staff or contributors without permission.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, close, or decline non-compliant issues or pull requests and remove contributors who repeatedly violate these standards.
