# Contributing to Awesome Assemblies of God

Thank you for helping make **Awesome Assemblies of God** the most complete, accurate, and accessible open-source directory of Assemblies of God churches, doctrines, educational institutions, missions, and ministry tools.

Please read our [Code of Conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md) before contributing.

## How to Contribute

1. **Check Existing Entries:** Search `README.md` (or the [live searchable directory](https://jsilvalabs.github.io/Awesome-Assemblies-of-God-Church/)) to verify the resource is not already listed.
2. **Fork & Branch:** Fork [`JSilvaLabs/Awesome-Assemblies-of-God-Church`](https://github.com/JSilvaLabs/Awesome-Assemblies-of-God-Church) and create a descriptive branch (e.g., `add-church-texas` or `fix-university-link`).
3. **Follow Quality & Link Standards:**
   - Use full `https://` URLs whenever available (never bare domains).
   - Provide a concise, factual one-line description with city/state for local churches.
   - Keep entries alphabetized within their table or list.
4. **Update `README.md` (and `index.html` data array if adding a primary card)** and submit a Pull Request with a brief summary of what you added or fixed.

## Inclusion Criteria

### For Churches
- **Biblical & Doctrinal Alignment:** Affirmation of the Statement of Fundamental Truths of the Assemblies of God.
- **Digital Accessibility:** Live website with current service times, campuses, or livestream/sermon archives.
- **Kingdom Impact:** Notable outreach, missions engagement, church planting, or community service.

### For Ministries, Higher Ed & Study Resources
- **Relevance & Reputation:** Verifiable AG affiliation, accredited academic standing, or sound Pentecostal/evangelical scholarship.
- **Open Utility:** Free, well-maintained, and directly useful to pastors, seminary students, educators, or believers.
